# CardioScope demo storyboard (about 4:50)

- **Format:** English narration over the real frozen V1 app (design update) using only the public development example and one clearly labeled hypothetical edit of it. The official requirement is a 3–10 minute YouTube video.
- **Setup:** follow [DEMO_REHEARSAL.md](DEMO_REHEARSAL.md).
- **On screen throughout:** the disclaimer bar. Let the screen carry caveats, units and fingerprints; the narration below covers only what judges need to hear.

| Time | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | "Click an artery on a real heart, and its model output and the reasons behind it follow. This is CardioScope." | **Prepared state:** example predicted, anterior heart, nothing selected. Click the exposed **LAD** on the heart. LAD glows, the others fade, the LAD card shows *Selected* and the readout shows 24.3%. |
| 0:15–0:40 | "CardioScope runs four frozen classifiers, for overall coronary artery disease and stenosis in the LAD, LCX and RCA, on 55 structured inputs from the public UCI Z-Alizadeh Sani dataset. Outputs, anatomy, explanations and evidence live in one workflow. It's an educational prototype, and the disclaimer stays on screen." | Scroll to the top: headline, the five-step guide and the four output cards. |
| 0:40–1:05 | "This is a public development record, not a patient. All 55 fields are explicit, with no hidden defaults or guessed units. Run prediction: FastAPI validates the profile and runs four frozen scikit-learn pipelines. Each card shows a model-estimated probability and the class at the fixed 0.5 threshold. Here, all four are Normal." | Click **Load development example**, then **Run prediction**. Point at the cards (17.1 / 24.3 / 37.1 / 30.9 %) without reading every number. |
| 1:05–1:50 | "These are 83 verified BodyParts3D meshes in one coordinate system. Each whole artery takes its own model's probability on one continuous scale, with gold at the threshold, and the legend shows where each vessel sits. Selection works both ways: click geometry and the card follows; pick a card and the camera finds the artery. This is reference anatomy. It doesn't reconstruct this person's heart or locate a blockage." | Click the **LAD** mesh. | 1:50–2:30 | "Why 17 percent? SHAP breaks the CAD output into signed contributions. Family history pushes it up. Typical chest pain, entered as zero, is the largest single factor pushing it down. **Optional cold open.** In editing, you may place the first 8 seconds of the 2:30 segment (LAD turning gold) before 0:00. Keep the on-screen *Hypothetical variation* label in frame.

**Do not:**
- read decimals, fingerprints or residuals aloud;
- explain log-odds versus probability units (the panel labels them);
- describe the edit as a patient, a treatment effect or a cause;
- demonstrate V2/V2.1 models.

**Rules:** record the actual app, never stills, as proof of interaction. Export with English audio or subtitles and check the final duration. Upload as public or unlisted only after explicit approval.
